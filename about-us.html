Elite Gear

Elite gear is a small Irish business that focuses on making high-quality Gaelic football gear. The company started in 2018 as a small business creating gloves and basic training equipment for local clubs. Over time, they expanded their range to include boots, teamwear, protective equipment, and customised club gear. What began as a small workshop has grown into a trusted brand for players and coaches who want reliable, Gaelic-specific products. Elite gear has a team of about 12 employees working across design, production, customer support, and online sales, and the company continues to grow as more clubs and players look for specialised Gaelic football equipment.

We are located in the heart of Dublin, Ireland. Our facility is equipped with the latest technology to ensure we produce the best quality gear for our customers. We welcome visitors to our showroom, where you can see our products firsthand and speak with our knowledgeable staff.
